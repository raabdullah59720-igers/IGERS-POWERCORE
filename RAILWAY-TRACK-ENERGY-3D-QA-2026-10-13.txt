BUGS FOUND AND FIXED
1. Geometry/contact alignment: In the 2D fallback, the harvester pad cuboid's top face was inside the sleeper instead of meeting its underside. Corrected the lower z coordinate so the pad top meets the sleeper underside. In the WebGL model, pad top alignment now follows the loaded sleeper underside instead of leaving a visual gap during compression.
2. Reset/session accounting: Resetting while a wheel was already approaching or loading a pad could immediately recount that in-flight pass. Reset now latches zones already in contact range; the same train contact will not be credited again until a fresh pass.
3. Mixed energy assumptions: Session Wh totals could combine values calculated with different input assumptions. Changing a valid energy scenario now clears its simulated session counters; invalid input suppresses calculated/live Wh and clears totals that used assumptions no longer considered valid.
4. WebGL performance: The GPU renderer previously requested a new frame continuously even when its panel was hidden or the simulation was paused. It now sleeps off-screen/when the tab is hidden, stops continuous rendering when paused, and redraws once when inputs or camera controls change. Render errors stop retrying every frame and keep the 2D fallback available.
5. Release metadata: HTML release metadata, asset query strings and service-worker revision now consistently identify railway v6 (previous metadata incorrectly said v2 while assets reported v5).

ENGINEERING BOUNDARIES
No real train feed, rail load cell, displacement sensor or battery telemetry is connected. Energy values are assumption-based; the contact length and pad deformation are illustrative. Friction loss is kept separate from electrical recovery. This is a conceptual visualization—not a calibrated railway digital twin, finite-element analysis, certification or operational safety system. No device should be installed on active railway infrastructure without railway-authority approval and professional structural/fatigue/signalling/electrical safety validation.
