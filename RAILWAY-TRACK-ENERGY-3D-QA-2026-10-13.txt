CALCULATOR / MOCKED CANVAS SMOKE TEST — PASS
Formulae:
  E_event,module = E_mechanical_available,event,module × conversion_efficiency
  E_day = E_event,module × passages_per_day_per_module × module_count × system_availability
  E_year = E_day × operating_days_per_year / 1000
  P_calendar_average = E_year(kWh) × 1000 / (365 × 24)
  battery_autonomy = battery_nameplate_kWh × usable_DoD × discharge_efficiency × 1000 / load_W

Default editable assumptions:
- Mechanical energy available at one module per train pass: 5 Wh (placeholder; must be measured).
- Electrical conversion efficiency: 35%.
- System availability: 90%.
- Train passages per day at each module: 40.
- Active modules: 4.
- Operating days/year: 365.
- Example load: 25 W.
- Battery nameplate capacity: 2 kWh; usable DoD 85%; discharge efficiency 92%.

Expected / checked scenario outputs:
- Electrical energy per active pass per module: 1.75 Wh.
- Energy across the selected modules per operating day after availability: 252 Wh/day.
- Annual energy at 365 operating days: 91.98 kWh/year.
- Calendar-year average power: 10.50 W.
- Equivalent daily run-time at 25 W load: 10.1 h/day.
- Battery autonomy under the entered battery assumptions: 62.6 h.
- Changing conversion efficiency to 50% recalculated daily / annual output to 360 Wh/day and 131.4 kWh/year.
- 300 operating days at 50% efficiency and 90% availability recalculated annual output to 108 kWh/year.
- Fractional train-passage count is rejected instead of silently rounded; 0% availability returns 0 Wh/day.
- Pause / resume handlers toggle status in isolated test.

ENGINEERING BOUNDARIES
- These outputs are scenario estimates. `5 Wh mechanical energy available per pass per module` is a placeholder for experimentally measured extractable energy at the harvester interface, not the train's total kinetic energy and not a claimed field result.
- No live railway timetable, physical sensor, track strain/vibration measurement or generator telemetry is connected.
- The canvas is an animated isometric 3D-style schematic, and the gallery SVG is a concept illustration; neither is a validated structural model, finite-element simulation, fabrication drawing or railway operations tool.
- Before any physical trial, a railway authority and qualified engineers must review rail/fastener/sleeper interface, track geometry, structural fatigue, vibration limits, signalling/electrical isolation, worker safety, maintenance access, net-energy effect and operational approval.
