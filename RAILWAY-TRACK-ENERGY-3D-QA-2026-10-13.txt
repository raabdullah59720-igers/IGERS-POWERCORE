MODEL BOUNDARY
This is an animated canvas/isometric engineering concept and input-driven calculator, not a calibrated WebGL model, finite-element analysis, or operational digital twin. Pad displacement is a visual input and does not independently predict Wh. Actual train wheel loads, track dynamics, transducer force-displacement behaviour, rectifier/storage losses, contact slip and temperatures must be measured and validated. Do not install equipment on an active railway without railway-authority approval and qualified track, fatigue, signalling, electrical-isolation and maintenance review.
