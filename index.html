01 · What is IGERS?

Core philosophy, output and engineering limits

The idea

Look where energy is already moving.
IGERS explores selected recovery points inside existing infrastructure instead of assuming every available energy source is fully recoverable.

The output

Recover → convert → store → use.
Recovered energy is conditioned, stored, monitored and routed to suitable nearby loads.

The limit

Engineering decides what is practical.
Mass, speed, elevation, flow, losses, structural constraints, safety, maintenance and economics control real output.
