IMPORTANT LIMITATION
A normal browser/PWA on GitHub Pages cannot create/manage a system VPN tunnel. Direct connect/disconnect needs the native Android app or Windows desktop host from the source bundles, plus a genuine WireGuard .conf profile from a trusted provider or a server under the user's control. The bundle includes no VPN server, endpoint credentials, private keys, subscription or free bandwidth. A running tunnel interface/service alone does not prove a peer handshake or working internet. No 100% success guarantee is claimed without native builds, provider configuration and device testing.
