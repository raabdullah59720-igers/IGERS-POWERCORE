BUGS FOUND AND FIXED
1. Malformed local records without a unique ID were previously accepted by the Scenario Vault. Such records could share selection state or cause a delete operation to remove multiple malformed records. v7 requires a well-formed record ID, deduplicates stored records, validates the scene against the 11 known IDs, and strictly validates numeric inputs and metrics.
2. Storage-quota/write failures could leave in-memory record lists changed while localStorage stayed unchanged. v7 uses a transactional commit: it writes first, then updates UI/state only after storage succeeds. Failed save/import/delete/clear operations leave the visible/persisted list intact.
3. Scenario names were written into CSV without spreadsheet formula-injection mitigation. v7 prefixes formula-like text cells before CSV quoting.
