Privacy & Data Use

This policy explains how the IGERS-BD-01 website handles information when you use its public interface, live weather/environmental features, notifications and related browser functionality.

Effective date: 13 September 2026 · Version 1.0

This is a website privacy notice, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice. If the service is later expanded with accounts, payments, analytics, forms or other data collection, this policy should be updated before those features are enabled.

1. Information the website may process

  • Browser location: if you grant browser geolocation permission, the site may receive latitude/longitude from your device to provide location-aware weather and environmental information. The site also maintains a safe fallback location when permission is unavailable.
  • Local browser storage: the site may store limited preferences locally, such as earthquake-notification settings and identifiers used to prevent duplicate alerts. This information is stored in your browser rather than submitted as an account profile.
  • Notification permission: if you enable browser notifications, the browser manages the permission state. The site uses it only for the alert feature described in the interface.
  • Technical request data: normal web requests may expose standard technical information such as IP address, user agent and timestamps to the hosting provider or external data-service provider. The site itself does not create a user account from this information.

2. External live-data providers

Live features can request data from third-party services. Depending on the feature and availability, these may include Open-Meteo for weather/environmental forecasts, USGS and EMSC for earthquake feeds, and Airplanes.live for ADS-B-derived air-traffic information. Their own privacy terms and technical policies apply to requests made to their infrastructure.

The website does not claim ownership of third-party datasets and does not represent third-party providers as affiliated with IGERS POWERCORE TECHNOLOGIES LTD.

3. How location information is used

When location access is granted, coordinates are used to request location-relevant weather/environmental information and to update the dashboard's displayed location state. The public interface is designed so that location permission can be denied; the site remains usable with its fallback behavior.

Do not grant location permission if you do not want your browser to provide location coordinates to the website and its relevant live-data requests.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current public package is designed without a mandatory user account, advertising profile or third-party behavioral analytics system. It may use browser local storage for functional preferences. If analytics, advertising, cookies requiring consent, account systems or other tracking technologies are added later, the privacy notice should be revised and any required consent mechanism implemented.

5. Data sharing and sale

The current website does not provide a user-data marketplace and does not state that personal information is sold. Functional requests to external live-data providers are a technical consequence of using those features and are governed by the providers' own systems and policies.

6. Security and limitations

Reasonable technical measures are used in the website implementation, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. The site should not be used to submit passwords, payment-card information, identity documents or other sensitive information through an unprovided form.

7. Children and general audience

The website is an engineering and environmental information interface. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians should supervise use of browser location and notification permissions where appropriate.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's functionality, data flows or legal requirements change. The effective date and version above should be updated when a material revision is published.
